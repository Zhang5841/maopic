# Persistent Illustration Style

## User intent
花间醒希望以后无论输入什么主题，都沿用一套固定的配色、构图和插画质感；主题本身可以变化，但视觉系统保持稳定。

## Visual decisions
- Palette: pale oat paper ground; coral/terracotta; mustard yellow; cornflower blue; muted teal/sage; restrained berry; charcoal details.
- Composition: one readable focal subject, 45-70% canvas occupancy, generous negative space, flat supporting shapes, gentle asymmetry, stable margins.
- Texture: opaque gouache on lightly toothy paper, visible brush edges, restrained pigment variation and print-like grain.
- Subject handling: simplify arbitrary topics into friendly organic silhouettes and a few topic-specific motifs; do not force cat imagery into unrelated prompts.
- Originality: use broad visual characteristics only; never include a real artist's name in prompts, metadata, or delivery notes; do not copy a reference's pose, crop, lettering, character, or arrangement.
